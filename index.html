Part II. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal data is collected, processed, and protected in Peakest (developed by Uranium Forest), in strict compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union.

1. Data Controller

  • Development Owner / Developer Name: Uranium Forest (Independent Developer)
  • Application: Peakest
  • Contact, Privacy and Support channel: uraniumforest@gmail.com

For any questions regarding the processing of your personal data or the exercise of your rights, you can contact us directly by email.

2. Collected Personal Data and Purpose

a. User account and authentication (Google Sign-In & AWS Cognito)

We use Google's secure sign-in system linked to AWS Cognito. We only collect the unique Google identifier (Google ID) and the associated email address, necessary to authenticate and protect your session.

Each user freely chooses a public username that will be displayed in the peak rankings and next to any comments or reviews they choose to publish.

b. GPS location and peak validation

Peakest operates under a strict principle of geolocation data minimization:

  • Local processing: The periodic check of proximity to peaks and the tracing of routes on the map run locally on the device. Your continuous real-time coordinates are not transmitted or stored permanently on external servers.
  • Conquered peak log: Only when the application verifies that you are physically within the radius of a peak, the confirmation of the conquered peak is sent to the cloud along with the date and time to assign the points to your ranking.
  • GPX route recording: Recorded routes are stored locally in the device's private storage and can be exported or deleted by the user.

c. Offline maps

Offline map packages are hosted on our cloud storage infrastructure (Cloudflare R2) and are downloaded and stored directly in your mobile device's local memory to allow network-free navigation.

d. Custom Background Images and Imported GPX Files

When you select an image from your gallery to customize an achievement card or import a GPX file, the file is processed exclusively locally within your device's temporary cache/storage. Your personal photos and GPX files are never uploaded to or stored on Uranium Forest's servers.

3. Infrastructure Providers and Recipients

Your personal data is not sold to third parties or used for unrelated commercial purposes. We solely use essential providers to deliver the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Database (DynamoDB), serverless functions (AWS Lambda), authentication management (AWS Cognito), and automated moderation/system notifications (AWS SNS), hosted on servers within the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden – eu-north-1) under GDPR security standards.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2): Cloud object storage used to host and distribute the offline map packages available for download in the application (generated from OpenStreetMap data extracted via Geofabrik.de).
  • Google Identity Services & Google Play: Account authentication and billing.
  • RevenueCat: Management and synchronization of subscription and purchase states.
  • OpenFreeMap & MapLibre: Vector map rendering engine (MapLibre) and online base map tiles (OpenFreeMap) when browsing in online mode.

Account and Personal Data Deletion Request (Google Play Policy)

In compliance with Google Play Developer policies and the European GDPR, users of Peakest (developed by Uranium Forest) have full control over their personal data and can request the complete and permanent deletion of their account at any time.

  1. From within the Peakest Mobile App (Instant):

  2. Via Email (Web / External Request):

Data Deletion vs. Retention Breakdown:

  • Locally Stored Data: Offline maps, recorded GPX tracks, and cached background images reside solely on your device storage; uninstalling the app or clearing its app data will erase this information completely.
  • Data Kept & Retention Period: We do not keep any backup copy of your personal identity once deleted. Anonymized transactional receipts (associated with Google Play purchases and managed by RevenueCat) are retained for a maximum of 5 years exclusively to satisfy mandatory Spanish tax and accounting obligations (Ley General Tributaria). These financial records cannot be used to recreate or access your account.

Peakest opera bajo un principio estricto de minimización de datos de geolocalización:

  • Procesamiento local: La comprobación periódica de proximidad a las cumbres y el trazado de rutas en el mapa se ejecutan localmente en el dispositivo. Sus coordenadas continuas en tiempo real no se transmiten ni se almacenan de forma permanente en servidores externos.
